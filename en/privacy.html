Privacy Policy

Galaxy Yatzy and bautapixel.se · Last updated 2 November 2026

Galaxy Yatzy collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code, and it never connects to the internet. This website sets no cookies and makes no third-party requests.

The app

What is stored on your device

Galaxy Yatzy saves two things in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • The game in progress, so you can close the app and pick it up where you left off.
  • The player names from the last game, so the next game starts with the same players.

Both stay on your device. Neither is sent anywhere, and both are removed when you delete the app.

Motion

Shake-to-roll reads your device's motion sensor while you are shaking, so it can tell a shake from ordinary handling. The readings are used in that moment to roll the dice, and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs — the app is built entirely from Apple's own frameworks.
  • No network connection. The app works with the device offline and never requests access to the internet.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share or sell, and nothing to request access to or have deleted.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

bautapixel.se is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and makes no requests to third parties — fonts, styles and images are all served from this domain.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub's servers process the IP address of each request in order to deliver the page, which is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. The app does not need to be updated for that to happen.
