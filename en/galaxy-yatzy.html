Galaxy Yatzy

Yatzy the way it is played at the kitchen table — only in your pocket. Three rule variants, as many players as you like, and dice you roll by shaking the phone.

For iPhone

One game, three variants

Everyone has their own yatzy. The variant is chosen when the game starts and sets the number of dice, the categories and the bonus.

  • Nordic

    Scandinavian rules with 15 categories, including One Pair and Two Pairs. Most combinations score their matching dice.

    Five dice · 50 pt bonus

  • International

    Yahtzee-style with 13 categories and no pair categories. Full House scores 25 and straights award fixed points.

    Five dice · 35 pt bonus

  • Maxi

    Six dice and 20 categories, adding Three Pairs, Five of a Kind, Full Straight, Villa and Tower.

    Six dice · 100 pt bonus

Scoring order

Beyond the variant, you choose how freely the categories may be filled in — the same house rules you already settle at the table, built in.

  • Free

    Score any unscored category you like on each turn. Standard rules.

  • Sequential

    Categories must be filled from top to bottom. Only the first unscored category is available each turn.

  • Follow

    The first player each round picks the category, and everyone else that round must score the same one.

Built to be played together

  • Shake to roll

    Shake the device and the dice rattle — with sound and haptics that keep going as long as you shake. Or just tap the button.

  • Around one screen

    Add as many players as you are, name them and drag them into order. The names are still there for the next game.

  • Undo

    Filled in the wrong box? Undo the last action and carry on — without restarting the game.

  • The game keeps

    A game in progress is saved locally and resumes where you left off, even after the app has been closed.

  • The scorecard up front

    The whole scorecard stays visible, with sums, bonus and how far you still are from the bonus.

  • Accessible

    Dice, boxes and standings are labelled for VoiceOver, so the game can be followed without seeing the screen.
